# Pengiraan pH

Formula:

pH = − log [H⁺]

Hubungan penting:

[H⁺] = 10⁻pH

---

## Contoh 1

### Soalan

Hitungkan nilai pH jika kepekatan ion hidrogen ialah  
0.5 mol dm⁻³.

---

### Diberi

[H⁺] = 0.5 mol dm⁻³

---

### Formula

pH = − log [H⁺]

---

### Pengiraan

log (0.5) = −0.301

pH = − log (0.5)

pH = − (−0.301)

pH = 0.301

---

### Jawapan

pH = **0.30**

---

## Contoh 2

### Soalan

Tentukan kemolaran HCl jika pH = 2.

---

### Diberi

pH = 2

---

### Formula

[H⁺] = 10⁻pH

---

### Pengiraan

[H⁺] = 10⁻²

[H⁺] = 0.01 mol dm⁻³

---

### Jawapan

Kemolaran HCl = **0.01 mol dm⁻³**

---

# Pengiraan pOH

Formula:

pOH = − log [OH⁻]

Hubungan penting:

[OH⁻] = 10⁻pOH

---

## Contoh

### Soalan

Hitungkan nilai pOH jika  
[OH⁻] = 0.1 mol dm⁻³.

---

### Formula

pOH = − log [OH⁻]

---

### Pengiraan

log (0.1) = −1

pOH = − (−1)

pOH = 1

---

### Jawapan

**pOH = 1**

---

# Hubungan pH dan pOH

Formula penting:

pH + pOH = 14

---

## Contoh

### Soalan

Hitungkan nilai pH jika pOH = 2.

---

### Pengiraan

pH + pOH = 14

pH + 2 = 14

pH = 14 − 2

pH = 12

---

### Jawapan

**pH = 12**

---

# Pengiraan Kepekatan (g dm⁻³)

Formula:

Kepekatan = jisim ÷ isipadu

Unit isipadu mesti dalam dm³.

---

## Contoh

### Soalan

15 g CuSO₄ dilarutkan dalam 750 cm³ larutan.

Hitungkan kepekatan dalam g dm⁻³.

---

### Penyelesaian

Penukaran unit:

750 cm³ = 0.75 dm³

---

### Pengiraan

Kepekatan = jisim ÷ isipadu

= 15 ÷ 0.75

= 20 g dm⁻³

---

### Jawapan

**20 g dm⁻³**

---

# Pengiraan Kemolaran

Formula:

M = n ÷ V

Di mana:

M = kemolaran (mol dm⁻³)  
n = bilangan mol (mol)  
V = isipadu larutan (dm³)

---

## Contoh

### Soalan

54.5 g ZnCl₂ dilarutkan dalam 5 dm³ larutan.

Hitungkan kemolaran larutan.

---

### Langkah 1: Hitung jisim molar ZnCl₂

Zn = 65.5  
Cl = 35.5  

Jisim molar ZnCl₂

= 65.5 + 2(35.5)

= 136.5 g mol⁻¹

---

### Langkah 2: Hitung bilangan mol

Mol = jisim ÷ jisim molar

= 54.5 ÷ 136.5

= 0.40 mol

---

### Langkah 3: Hitung kemolaran

M = n ÷ V

= 0.40 ÷ 5

= 0.08 mol dm⁻³

---

### Jawapan

**0.08 mol dm⁻³**

---

# Pengiraan Pencairan

Formula:

M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

---

## Contoh

### Soalan

Larutan HNO₃ 20 mol dm⁻³ digunakan untuk menyediakan  
75 cm³ larutan 4 mol dm⁻³.

Hitungkan isipadu larutan asal yang diperlukan.

---

### Pengiraan

M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

20 × V₁ = 4 × 75

V₁ = (4 × 75) ÷ 20

V₁ = 15 cm³

---

### Jawapan

**15 cm³**

---

# Pengiraan Pentitratan

---

## Contoh 1

### Soalan

20 cm³ larutan NaOH 0.25 mol dm⁻³ dititratkan dengan HCl  
0.1 mol dm⁻³.

Hitungkan isipadu HCl yang diperlukan.

---

### Persamaan kimia

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

---

### Langkah 1: Hitung mol NaOH

Mol = M × V

= 0.25 × (20 ÷ 1000)

= 0.005 mol

---

### Langkah 2: Nisbah mol

HCl : NaOH = 1 : 1

Mol HCl = 0.005 mol

---

### Langkah 3: Hitung isipadu HCl

V = n ÷ M

= 0.005 ÷ 0.1

= 0.05 dm³

= 50 cm³

---

### Jawapan

**50 cm³**

---

## Contoh Pentitratan 2

### Soalan

4.05 g asid oksalik digunakan untuk meneutralkan  
50 cm³ larutan NaOH.

Hitungkan kemolaran NaOH.

---

### Langkah 1: Hitung jisim molar asid oksalik

H₂C₂O₄ = 90 g mol⁻¹

---

### Langkah 2: Hitung mol asid oksalik

Mol = jisim ÷ jisim molar

= 4.05 ÷ 90

= 0.045 mol

---

### Langkah 3: Gunakan nisbah persamaan kimia

Persamaan:

H₂C₂O₄ + 2NaOH → Na₂C₂O₄ + 2H₂O

Nisbah mol:

1 mol H₂C₂O₄ : 2 mol NaOH

Mol NaOH

= 2 × 0.045

= 0.090 mol

---

### Langkah 4: Hitung kemolaran NaOH

Isipadu NaOH

50 cm³ = 0.05 dm³

M = n ÷ V

= 0.090 ÷ 0.05

= 1.8 mol dm⁻³

---

### Jawapan

**1.8 mol dm⁻³**
