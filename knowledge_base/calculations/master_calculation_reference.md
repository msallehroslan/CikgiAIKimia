# 1. PEMALAR KIMIA PENTING

## Nombor Avogadro

NA = 6.02 × 10²³ mol⁻¹

Digunakan untuk menukar antara:

- mol
- bilangan zarah (atom / molekul / ion)

Formula:

Bilangan zarah = mol × NA

---

## Isi Padu Molar Gas

Pada keadaan standard (STP):

1 mol gas = **22.4 dm³**

Pada keadaan bilik:

1 mol gas = **24 dm³**

---

## Muatan Haba Tentu Air

c = **4.2 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹**

Digunakan dalam pengiraan kalorimetri.

---

# 2. PENUKARAN UNIT PENTING

1 dm³ = 1000 cm³

1000 mg = 1 g

1 kJ = 1000 J

1 cm³ larutan akueus ≈ 1 g

---

# 3. FORMULA KONSEP MOL

Mol dan jisim:

n = m ÷ M

di mana:

n = bilangan mol  
m = jisim (g)  
M = jisim molar (g mol⁻¹)

---

Mol dan bilangan zarah:

n = N ÷ NA

di mana:

N = bilangan zarah

---

Mol dan isi padu gas:

n = V ÷ Vm

di mana:

V = isi padu gas  
Vm = isi padu molar gas

---

# 4. FORMULA LARUTAN

Kemolaran:

M = n ÷ V

di mana:

M = kemolaran (mol dm⁻³)  
n = bilangan mol  
V = isipadu larutan (dm³)

---

Kepekatan:

Kepekatan (g dm⁻³) = jisim ÷ isipadu

---

Formula pencairan:

M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

---

# 5. FORMULA pH DAN pOH

pH = − log [H⁺]

pOH = − log [OH⁻]

Hubungan:

pH + pOH = 14

---

# 6. FORMULA TERMOKIMIA

Kalorimetri:

Q = mcΔT

di mana:

Q = haba (J)  
m = jisim larutan (g)  
c = muatan haba tentu air  
ΔT = perubahan suhu

---

Perubahan entalpi:

ΔH = Q ÷ mol

Unit:

kJ mol⁻¹

---

# 7. FORMULA KADAR TINDAK BALAS

Kadar tindak balas purata:

Kadar = perubahan kuantiti ÷ masa

Kecerunan graf:

Kadar = Δy ÷ Δx

---

# 8. PERATURAN NOMBOR PENGOKSIDAAN

1. Unsur bebas = 0

2. Ion monoatom = cas ion

3. Hidrogen biasanya = +1

4. Oksigen biasanya = −2

5. Jumlah nombor pengoksidaan bagi sebatian neutral = 0

6. Jumlah nombor pengoksidaan bagi ion = cas ion
